Maintain05 · built to change

Manage the pieces. Extend the comfort life.

RotateFlipRepositionReplace

A traditional mattress gives you one maintenance move: rotate the whole thing. TogetherBed lets you manage components individually as wear or needs change.

Rotatechange component orientation
Flipuse the opposite surface
Repositionmove a piece to a new zone
Replaceonly when a piece needs it

Managing individual components can help distribute wear before replacement is necessary. When a piece finally does wear out, replace the piece, not the entire mattress.

    365-Day Comfort Adjustment

    No mattress return required. If your comfort needs adjustment during the first 365 days, TogetherBed will send a replacement comfort piece. You pay only the cost of shipping.

    TogetherBed mattresses are not taken back under this comfort-adjustment policy. The goal is to adjust the part of the bed that needs to change rather than replace the entire mattress.
